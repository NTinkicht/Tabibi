# Retired Tabibi Actors

## 2026-09-11 — Gemini Agent and Gemini Chat

Owner decision: `gemini_agent` and `gemini_chat` are retired from the Tabibi operating model.

Consequences:

- no new role leases, work units, review/gating authority, capacity probes or wakeups;
- no participation requirements in standups, retrospectives, Slack or Coffee Corner;
- no active Slack/GitHub automation path should route work to them;
- historical comments, reviews, commits and generated records remain valid project history and must not be rewritten or deleted merely because the actors are retired;
- any future reintroduction would require a new explicit owner decision plus a reviewed governance change. `CAPACITY_RECOVERED` alone cannot restore a retired actor.

Their former runtime-specific instruction/workflow/Slack-manifest files are removed as part of the same Company OS v2 change.
