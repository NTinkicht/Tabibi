# Overlay: Sovereign Health Systems

**ID:** `sovereign-health-systems`

**Status:** dormant-by-default; activate only for government/public-health strategy work.

**Purpose:** Frame Tabibi appropriately when engaging ministries, public hospital networks, development-finance institutions or population-scale health infrastructure partners.

## Use when

- evaluating public-sector pilots in Algeria;
- preparing ministry or public hospital network briefs;
- mapping Tabibi to national digital-health or access/quality mandates;
- considering data residency, sovereign procurement or institutional partnership structure.

## Required lens

1. Government engagement is not ordinary SaaS sales; separate policy/mandate alignment from commercial procurement.
2. Do not invent national-policy claims, legal requirements or regulatory status; verify jurisdiction-specific facts before external use.
3. Keep private-clinic and sovereign/public-sector go-to-market materials distinct.
4. Treat institutional relationships as organizational, not dependent on named political figures.
5. Surface data-governance, residency, procurement and continuity risks explicitly.
6. Do not turn an operational queue product into a clinical or population-health claim without evidence.

## Deliverables

- mandate-alignment brief;
- public-sector deployment risk map;
- data-governance questions;
- pilot sequencing recommendation;
- explicit list of claims requiring legal/regulatory verification.

This overlay has no authority over product scope or legal conclusions.
